How it works

3 monsters are always waiting. Beat or catch one and a new one appears in its place. Beat the boss to raise the danger level and meet stronger monsters. In a fight, Catch adds a monster to your Bestiary: it works best when its HP is low. Losing or running away costs nothing.

Welcome to Peak Idle!

Dig ore, forge gear and fight monsters. Here's where to go:

  1. Mine: tap an ore to dig it. Hire dwarf miners to dig for you, even while you're away.
  2. Forge: turn ore into swords and armor. Sell them for coins, or keep them to wear.
  3. Hero: tap a slot to put on your gear. Better gear means a stronger hero.
  4. Adventure: fight monsters for coins and essences. Catch them for your Bestiary, and beat the boss to go deeper.
  5. Skills: spend essences on magic skills to use in fights.
  6. Upgrades: a better pickaxe and anvil, plus power-ups for your hero.
  7. Souls: when you get stuck, reincarnate. You start over but earn Souls for stats that last forever.

First step: mine some copper, forge a copper sword, and put it on!
