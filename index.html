Mine

How it works

Click an ore to mine it. Hire miners to mine while you're away.

Forge

How it works

Turn ore into swords and armor. Sell them for coins, or equip them to make your hero stronger. Sold gear is gone for good.

Hero

How it works

Your stats go up with the gear you wear and the Soul attributes you buy. Heavy armor gives more defense, light armor more agility. Tap an equipment slot to pick what to wear from your bag.

Adventure

How it works

3 monsters are always waiting. Beat or catch one and a new one appears in its place. Beat the boss to raise the danger level and meet stronger monsters. In a fight, Catch adds a monster to your Bestiary: it works best when its HP is low. Losing or running away costs nothing.

Upgrades

How it works

Tools are bought with coins. Element Mastery is bought with essence and is permanent. Run Boons cost ore plus essence and boost your stats until you reincarnate.

Souls

How it works

Reincarnate to start a new run and earn Souls. Spend Souls on attributes: they add to your hero's stats forever, even after you reincarnate again.
